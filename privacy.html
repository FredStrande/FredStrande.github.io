Privacy policy

tpop analytics is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @thepriceofperspective to read that channel's own statistics.

  • It requests read-only access (YouTube Analytics and YouTube Data API) and never changes anything on YouTube.
  • It reads only aggregated channel and video statistics (views, likes, retention), no data about individual viewers.
  • The numbers are stored in the owner's private repository and are not shared with or sold to anyone.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
